\noindent To the Editors, \textit{The World Bank Economic Review}\\[1em]
\noindent Dear Editors,\\[0.5em]
I submit the manuscript ``When Husbands Stop Working, Do Wives Start? Evidence from India's Urban Labour Force Panel'' for consideration as a Short Paper.

The paper asks whether wives in urban India enter the labour force when their husbands stop working. It links couples across consecutive quarterly visits of the urban rotational panel of India's Periodic Labour Force Survey, joining revisits across the 2022--23 and 2023--24 annual files, and replicates the analysis on the 2017--18 panel. The entry response of wives is small and statistically indistinguishable from zero, while working wives are far more likely to stop working in the same quarter as their husbands. The paper makes a focused empirical point about how much insurance wives' labour supply provides to urban households.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and contains five exhibits. A supplementary file documents the panel linkage, the validation of the weighted estimates against published figures, and additional checks. The analysis uses PLFS unit-level data published by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Government of India. The data are available from the MoSPI microdata portal under its terms of use and cannot be redistributed by the author; the complete code and instructions for obtaining the data will be released publicly. There are no other restrictions on data or code.

The author has no conflicts of interest and received no funding for this work. AI tools, including Claude (Anthropic), were used as described in the manuscript's declaration.\\[1em]
