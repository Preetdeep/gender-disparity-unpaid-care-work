# Fascinating Research Questions Using India's Time Use Survey 2024

## Your Suggestions (Excellent!)

Based on your interest in 3-digit activity codes, here are refined versions of your questions plus many more:

### 1. **Gender of Head of Household and Educational Activities**
**Question**: Do children spend more time on educational activities when the household head is female vs. male?

**Variables**:
- Activity codes 21-29 (Learning and studying)
- Household head gender
- Children aged 6-18

**Hypothesis**: Female-headed households may prioritize children's education more, or have different time allocation patterns

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Matriarchy and Merit: How Female Household Leadership Shapes Children's Educational Time in India"*

---

### 2. **Marriage and Social Life: The Friendship Penalty**
**Question**: Do men spend less time with friends after marriage? What about women?

**Variables**:
- Activity codes 81-89 (Social life and communication)
- Specifically: 813 (Socializing and communication), 814 (Attending social events)
- Marital status × Gender

**Expected Finding**: Men's social time drops post-marriage; women's drops even more

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Marriage and the Friendship Penalty: Gender Asymmetries in Social Isolation"*

---

### 3. **The Television Penalty: Who Watches More After Marriage?**
**Question**: Does TV watching increase or decrease after marriage, and does it differ by gender?

**Variables**:
- Activity codes 73 (Audio and visual media)
- Specifically: 732 (Watching television and video)
- Marital status × Gender × Age

**Hypothesis**: Women watch less (more chores); men watch same/more (leisure time protected)

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Remote Control Dynamics: Marriage and the Gender Gap in Leisure Screen Time"*

## Additional Compelling Research Questions

### 4. **The Sleep Penalty: Gender Gaps in Rest and Recovery**
**Question**: Do married women sleep less than married men? How does care work burden affect sleep time?

**Variables**:
- Activity code 01 (Sleep and related activities)
- Care work burden × Gender × Marital status

**Why It Matters**: Sleep deprivation has health, productivity, and wellbeing consequences

**JFE-Style Title**: *"The Sleep Penalty: How Marriage and Care Work Deprive Indian Women of Rest"*

---

### 5. **Exercise and Physical Activity: The Gendered Health Cost of Marriage**
**Question**: Does marriage reduce women's (but not men's) time for exercise and physical activity?

**Variables**:
- Activity codes 71 (Physical exercise, sports, outdoors)
- Marital status × Gender × Age

**Expected**: Women's exercise time plummets post-marriage; men's unchanged

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Marriage and the Gender Gap in Physical Activity: Health Consequences of Unpaid Work"*

---

### 6. **Religious and Spiritual Activities: Gender Differences**
**Question**: Do women engage more in religious activities than men? Does this vary by marital status?

**Variables**:
- Activity code 9 (Religious and spiritual activities)
- Gender × Marital status × Age × Education

**Interesting Angle**: Is religion compensatory (coping with stress) or cultural (gender norms)?

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Faith and Gender: Religious Time Use as Response to Domestic Burden"*

---

### 7. **Commuting and Work Travel: The Hidden Time Tax**
**Question**: How much time do employed women vs. men spend commuting? Does proximity of home to work differ by gender?

**Variables**:
- Travel time to/from work (embedded in activity codes)
- Employment type × Gender × Urban/Rural

**Why It Matters**: Commute time compounds the double burden for employed women

**JFE-Style Title**: *"The Commute Penalty: Gender Gaps in Work-Related Travel Time"*

---

### 8. **Meal Preparation vs. Meal Consumption: Who Cooks, Who Eats?**
**Question**: Do women spend more time cooking than eating? How does this ratio differ from men?

**Variables**:
- Activity code 321 (Food preparation)
- Activity code 011 (Eating and drinking - if separate)
- Gender × Household composition

**Dark Hypothesis**: Women cook for hours, eat in minutes

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Feeding Others, Starving Time: Gender Gaps in Meal Preparation and Consumption"*

---

### 9. **The Education Paradox: Do Educated Women Face Larger Marriage Penalties?**
**Question**: Does higher education protect against or exacerbate the marriage penalty in care work?

**Variables**:
- Care work time × Marital status × Education level
- Test for interaction effects

**Competing Hypotheses**:
- Protection: Educated women have more bargaining power
- Exacerbation: Educated women marry educated men with traditional expectations

**JFE-Style Title**: *"When Education Fails: The Marriage Penalty Among India's College-Educated Women"*

---

### 10. **Childcare vs. Elder Care: Differential Gender Burdens**
**Question**: Are men more willing to help with childcare than elder care? What about women?

**Variables**:
- Activity code 31 (Childcare for household members)
- Activity code 32 (Care for adults in the household)
- Gender × Recipient of care

**Hypothesis**: Men avoid elder care even more than childcare

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Selective Care: Why Indian Men Help with Children but Not the Elderly"*

---

### 11. **Shopping and Household Management: The Mental Load**
**Question**: Who does the shopping, errand-running, and household management tasks?

**Variables**:
- Activity code 34 (Shopping for goods and services)
- Activity code 35 (Household management)
- Gender × Marital status

**Why Novel**: Captures "mental load" beyond physical work

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Beyond Physical Labor: The Gendered Mental Load of Household Management"*

---

### 12. **Volunteering and Community Participation by Gender**
**Question**: Do men have more time for community engagement and volunteering? How does marriage affect this?

**Variables**:
- Activity codes 51-59 (Unpaid volunteer/community service)
- Gender × Marital status × Education

**Hypothesis**: Men's civic engagement protected post-marriage; women's declines

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Marriage and Civic Disengagement: Why Women Disappear from Public Life"*

---

### 13. **The Weekend Effect: Is the Marriage Penalty Worse on Weekends?**
**Question**: Do gender gaps in care work widen on weekends when men are home?

**Variables**:
- Care work time × Gender × Marital status × Day of week
- Compare weekdays vs. weekends

**Provocative Hypothesis**: Men's presence increases women's workload (cooking for them, cleaning after them)

**JFE-Style Title**: *"Weekends and the Wife Penalty: When Husbands are Home, Women Work More"*

---

### 14. **Reading and Self-Improvement: The Gender Gap in Personal Development**
**Question**: Do women have less time for reading, learning new skills, or personal development?

**Variables**:
- Activity codes 21 (Reading)
- Activity codes 841 (Reading)
- Gender × Marital status × Education

**Connection**: Human capital accumulation doesn't end at formal schooling

**JFE-Style Title**: *"The Reading Gap: How Care Work Constrains Women's Lifelong Learning"*

---

### 15. **Eating While Working: The Simultaneity of Women's Time**
**Question**: Do women multi-task more (e.g., cook while caring for children)? Can we measure this using simultaneous activities?

**Variables**:
- Simultaneous activity codes (minor activities performed alongside major)
- Gender × Type of activities combined

**Methodological Note**: TUS captures simultaneous activities!

**JFE-Style Title**: *"The Multi-Tasking Penalty: Why Women's Time is Never Their Own"*
