# Paper 04: The Care Hidden in the Margins

**Target journal:** *Review of Economics of the Household*. It publishes measurement-oriented time-use economics, including secondary childcare (Kalenkoski and Foster 2008; Connelly and Kimmel 2009).
**Fallbacks:** *Feminist Economics*, *Journal of Time Use Research*, *Social Indicators Research*.

## Contribution in one line

This is the first India-wide measure of secondary activity, secondary childcare and leisure shared with care. It shows how much single-activity accounting understates women's work, which matters for SDG 5.4.1 and for valuations of unpaid work.

## Cover letter (draft)

> Dear Editor,
>
> I submit "The Care Hidden in the Margins" for consideration in *Review of Economics of the Household*.
>
> Standard time-use statistics, including SDG indicator 5.4.1, count one activity per time slot and so miss care done alongside other tasks. India's Time Use Survey 2024 records every activity in each half-hour slot and flags the main one. I use it to measure secondary activity, secondary childcare, meals eaten while working, and free time shared with care. I show how much counting secondary activity changes the measured gender gap in childcare and in unpaid work.
>
> The manuscript is original and not under review elsewhere. Replication code is public; the microdata are available from the National Statistics Office, Government of India.
