# Highlights (World Development: 3–5 bullets, max. 85 characters each including spaces)

- Women's paid work in India rose by 11 minutes a day between 2019 and 2024
- The rise came from more women working on a given day, not longer hours per worker
- Women's unpaid work did not fall: less cleaning was offset by more childcare
- The gender gap in unpaid work narrowed by only 3 of 292 minutes a day
- Composition changes do not explain the rise in women's paid work
