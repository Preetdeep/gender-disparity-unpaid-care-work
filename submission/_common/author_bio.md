**Biography:** Preet Deep Singh is an independent researcher based in New Delhi, India. He holds a PhD from the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad and studied at Delhi University. He has taught at the Indian Institute of Management Sambalpur, the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay and the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, and has worked with the government. He is a qualified Company Secretary. His research focuses on gender, time use and labour in India.
