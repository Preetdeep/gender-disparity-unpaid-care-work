# Paper 08: The Free-Time Gap over the Life Course

**Target journal:** *Social Indicators Research*. It publishes measures of well-being, leisure inequality and time poverty, including descriptive time-use work.
**Fallbacks:** *Journal of Time Use Research*, *Feminist Economics*, *Asian Population Studies*.

## Contribution in one line

The gender gap in free time and time poverty is traced across seven family stages and compared with variation by education, consumption, caste and religion. The paper consolidates twelve one-dimension working papers into a single framework.

## Cover letter (draft)

> Dear Editor,
>
> I submit "The Free-Time Gap over the Life Course" for consideration in *Social Indicators Research*.
>
> Gender gaps in free time are usually reported as one national average. Using every diary in India's Time Use Survey 2024, I trace the gap and the incidence of time poverty across seven family stages. I compare that life-course variation with variation by education, consumption, caste and religion, and show how the composition of men's and women's free time differs.
>
