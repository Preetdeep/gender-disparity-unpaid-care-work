DATA META-INFORMATION
Paper: "Married and Never-Married Days: Marriage, Gender and the Full Time Budget in India, 2024"

1. Source, access and licence
   Survey:     Time Use Survey (TUS) 2024, January-December 2024, India.
   Producer:   National Statistics Office (NSO), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation
               (MoSPI), Government of India.
   Where:      MoSPI microdata portal, https://microdata.gov.in (catalog entry 236). Registration on the
               portal is required; our copies of the files are in Stata (.dta) format.
   Licence:    Use is governed by the portal's terms of use for registered users. Redistribution of the
               unit-level files is not permitted, so none are included in this archive.
   Files used: (a) the person-level activity file (TimeUse106PER.dta in our copy): one row per activity
               episode of each household member, with the household roster fields for members without a
               diary; (b) the household file (tus106hh.dta in our copy), used for household
               characteristics (religion, social group, household size, consumption expenditure).
               File names on the portal may differ; the code resolves columns by name (see R/build_panel.R).

2. Records used (all computed from the data at run time, see panel_validation() in R/build_panel.R)
   Activity records (rows) used:                 10,211,478   (all rows; none sampled)
   Persons on the roster:                           533,719
   Persons with a diary (person-days) in the file:  450,457
   Households:                                      139,487
   First-stage units (FSUs, the clustering unit):     9,969
   Diaries of third-gender respondents (excluded from the women/men comparisons): 49
   Analysis sample for the paper:
     Women aged 20-39, never married or currently married:  88,337 diaries
     Men aged 20-39, never married or currently married:    87,364 diaries
     Linked couples (wife aged 20-59 with linked spouse diary): 106,858
     Widowed vs married, ages 50-79: sample size is computed in the widowhood chunk of analysis_05.R
   Person-day construction: minutes come from major activities only; all but 5 of the 450,457 diaries
   sum to exactly 1,440 minutes.

3. Variables used (raw name in the activity file -> meaning)
   Identifiers and design
     FSU_Serial_No         first-stage unit (primary sampling unit); standard errors are clustered on it
     Sample_HH_No          household serial number within the FSU
     Person_Serial_No      person serial number within the household
     Weight                survey multiplier used as the estimation weight
     State, Sector         state/UT code; urban-rural sector
     Day_of_Week, Type_of_Day   diary day of week; whether the diary day was normal
     Informant_Serial_No   respondent who reported the diary (own diary vs proxy report)
   Person characteristics
     Gender, Age           sex (1 male, 2 female, 3 third gender, per the survey dictionary); age in years
     Marital_Status        never married, currently married, widowed, divorced/separated
     Highest_Education     recoded to four tiers: not literate / below primary; primary / middle;
                           secondary / higher secondary; graduate and above (R/codes.R)
     Principal_Activity_Status   usual principal status; used for "employed" and "student"
     Relation_to_Head      relationship to household head (codes used: head, spouse of head, married
                           child of head, spouse of married child, parent of head)
   Household characteristics (household file or merged columns)
     Religion, Social_Group, Household_Size, consumption expenditure (quintiles of monthly per-capita)
   Activity episode
     Activity_Code_3Digit  ICATUS 2016 three-digit code, grouped into the nine ICATUS divisions and the
                           sub-activities (sleep, socializing, exercise and sport, religious practice,
                           mass media) in R/codes.R
     time_spent            minutes in the episode
     Major_Activity_Flag   1 = major activity, 2 = minor (only major activities are summed)
   Derived person-day variables used in the paper (constructed in R/build_panel.R)
     employment, ownuse_goods, unpaid_domestic, unpaid_care, volunteer, learning, social_all,
     leisure_all, selfcare_all (minutes per day in the nine divisions); sleep, socialising,
     exercise_sport, religious, mass_media (sub-rows); unpaid_total; married; edu_tier; urban;
     proxy_report; hh_n_parent_gen, hh_n_under6, hh_n_60plus (household composition from the roster);
     spouse_* variables (linked spouse's age, education, employment, minutes), built from the roster.
   Basic description of the main outcome variables (weighted means, women/men aged 20-39) is in
   Table 1 of the paper.

4. Small sample data set
   The restricted data cannot be shared. sample_data/sample_synthetic_activity_records.csv is FAKE
   (generated by R/make_synthetic_data.R, seed 7) and only shows the column layout.

5. Other data in the archive
   submission/_common/tus_2019_2024_compare.csv contains aggregate coefficients (no unit records) from
   the same harmonised specification applied to Time Use Survey 2019 (NSO, MoSPI), used for the 2019
   vs 2024 sentence in the Data section.
